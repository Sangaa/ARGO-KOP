# Purpose

This document defines how information moves throughout the ARGO KOP platform.

It establishes the principles governing information creation, transformation, validation, storage, retrieval, and long-term preservation.

Information flow shall remain structured, traceable, and architecture-driven.

---

# Scope

This model applies to every component within ARGO KOP.

Every document, decision, project, knowledge entry, and repository artifact participates in this information flow.

---

# Information Lifecycle

Information progresses through the following stages:

Reality

↓

Observation

↓

Documentation

↓

Validation

↓

Knowledge

↓

Decision

↓

Memory

↓

Repository

↓

Future Reuse

No information shall bypass any required validation stage.

---

# Information Sources

ARGO KOP recognizes multiple information sources.

• Human Experience

• Documents

• Projects

• Operational Activities

• Engineering Decisions

• External References

• Artificial Intelligence Outputs

Every source shall be identified whenever practical.

---

# Information Categories

Information is classified into:

Facts

Assumptions

Opinions

Decisions

Knowledge

Historical Records

Lessons Learned

Future Ideas

These categories shall never be mixed without clear distinction.

---

# Information Validation

Before becoming platform knowledge, information should be:

Verified

Documented

Classified

Referenced

Connected

Traceable

---

# Information Transformation

Information evolves according to the following model:

Raw Information

↓

Structured Information

↓

Validated Knowledge

↓

Reusable Knowledge

↓

Organizational Memory

Every transformation increases quality without losing traceability.

---

# Information Relationships

Information may create relationships such as:

Depends On

References

Extends

Supersedes

Supports

Conflicts With

These relationships shall be explicitly documented whenever applicable.

---

# Information Storage

Validated information shall be stored inside the appropriate repository component.

No information shall exist without an identifiable location.

Repository organization shall always reflect architectural boundaries.

---

# Information Retrieval

Information retrieval shall prioritize:

Accuracy

Architecture

Context

Relationships

Traceability

Historical Continuity

---

# Information Integrity

ARGO KOP protects information integrity through:

Governance

Version Control

Documentation Standards

Repository Structure

Architecture Reviews

Decision Logging

---

# Information Evolution

Information may evolve over time.

Historical knowledge shall be preserved.

Updated knowledge shall reference previous versions whenever required.

Knowledge evolution shall never destroy repository history.

---

# Information Flow Principles

Reality precedes documentation.

Documentation precedes knowledge.

Knowledge precedes decisions.

Decisions create memory.

Memory strengthens the repository.

The repository preserves organizational intelligence.

---

# Success Criteria

The information flow is considered successful when:

Knowledge remains accurate.

Information remains traceable.

Repository remains organized.

Historical context is preserved.

Future users can understand past decisions.
