# CODE OF CONDUCT

---

# ARGO KOP

Knowledge Operating Platform

Code of Conduct

---

## Purpose

This Code of Conduct defines the expected behavior for everyone who participates in the ARGO KOP project.

Its purpose is to maintain a professional, respectful, and engineering-focused environment where knowledge can evolve through constructive collaboration.

---

## Our Philosophy

ARGO KOP is built upon engineering discipline.

Ideas are evaluated by their quality.

Decisions are evaluated by their evidence.

People are always treated with respect.

---

## Core Values

Respect

Integrity

Honesty

Responsibility

Transparency

Continuous Learning

Constructive Collaboration

---

## Expected Behavior

Every participant is expected to:

Treat others with respect.

Communicate professionally.

Support evidence-based discussions.

Accept constructive feedback.

Share knowledge openly.

Document important decisions.

Help improve the platform.

Protect architectural consistency.

Promote long-term thinking over short-term convenience.

---

## Engineering Culture

ARGO KOP encourages:

Clear communication.

Critical thinking.

Technical discussions.

Architectural reviews.

Knowledge sharing.

Continuous improvement.

Professional disagreement based on facts.

---

## Disagreement Policy

Disagreement is a normal part of engineering.

When opinions differ:

Return to documented facts.

Review the architecture.

Evaluate evidence.

Consider long-term impact.

Respect the final decision.

The objective is to improve the platform—not to win an argument.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

Personal attacks.

Harassment.

Discrimination.

Hostile communication.

Intentional misinformation.

Destructive criticism.

Ignoring established governance.

Publishing confidential information without authorization.

Deliberately damaging repository integrity.

---

## Repository Responsibility

Every contributor shares responsibility for protecting:

Knowledge Integrity

Architectural Consistency

Documentation Quality

Repository Traceability

Platform Reputation

---

## Leadership Principles

Project maintainers should:

Lead by example.

Explain architectural decisions.

Encourage discussion.

Accept justified improvements.

Document important changes.

Protect the platform vision.

---

## Conflict Resolution

When conflicts occur:

Pause implementation.

Review the relevant documentation.

Evaluate architectural impact.

Seek consensus whenever possible.

If consensus cannot be reached, the repository owner has the final decision.

---

## Community Principle

Every contribution should increase the value of the repository.

Every discussion should increase understanding.

Every decision should strengthen the platform.

---

## Guiding Statement

Respect people.

Challenge ideas.

Protect knowledge.

Preserve architecture.

---

## Final Commitment

By participating in ARGO KOP, contributors agree to support an engineering culture based on respect, discipline, collaboration, and continuous improvement.

---

Knowledge Organized.

Decisions Preserved.

Intelligence Connected.

---

End of Code of Conduct
