## Current identity

ARGO KOP is presently best classified as a cognitive engineering / evidence-governance architecture with a prospective product path. It is **not yet market-proven**.

## Current strengths

- Evidence-first engineering discipline.
- Explicit separation of fact, assumption, unknown, unresolved and not-observable states.
- Reusable connector-boundary learning.
- Controlled self-correction rather than uncontrolled mutation.
- Relationship/graph-oriented architecture.
- Strong governance and provenance thinking.
- Ability to preserve learning across sessions.

## Current weaknesses / risks

- Full runtime production reachability is not universally proven.
- Some policy-correlation questions remain unresolved.
- Formal self-capability measurement is newly established and not yet mature.
- External customer validation is absent/not established by this assessment.
- Market traction is not established.
- Product UX and externally consumable assurance workflows remain to be proven.

## Compass

### Now

**Connected Baseline Stabilization + Self-Assurance foundation.**

### Next

**Candidate Seam Enumeration → Executable Evidence/Relationship Graph → Canonical Spine → Full Connectivity/GAP MAP → Assurance MVP.**

### Later

External validation, benchmark suite, policy/control packs, enterprise connectors, and productization only after the core assurance capability is reproducibly demonstrated.

## Red lines

- No self-congratulation as evidence.
- No market-superiority claim without external comparative evidence.
- No product-market-fit claim without users/customers.
- No runtime claim without runtime evidence.
- No policy PASS merely because tests pass.
- No mutation solely to turn an unresolved state into PASS.

## Success condition

The strongest future ARGO claim is not “ARGO is powerful.” It is:

> **ARGO can show, with current evidence, exactly what it can prove, what it cannot prove, why, and how that state changed over time.**
