# GOV-026 — HERMUZ Solution Evolution & Stability Protocol

Document ID: GOV-026
**Status:** PROPOSED — GOVERNANCE REVIEW REQUIRED
**Purpose:** Preserve ARGO's principle that solutions remain reviewable and improvable without forcing premature optimization, endless analysis, or indiscriminate doubt.
Identity migration: from colliding historical `GOV-017_HERMUZ_SOLUTION_EVOLUTION_AND_STABILITY_PROTOCOL.md`; Proposed status unchanged.

## 1. Core Principles

1. There is usually a simpler solution worth looking for.
2. Nothing is sacred; every solution remains reviewable.
3. Reviewability does not mean rejection of a practical solution.
4. Solve the current real problem first when a safe practical solution exists.
5. Do not optimize indefinitely when the current solution satisfies the actual need.
6. Every meaningful improvement should leave an evolution record.
7. Uncertainty is local unless evidence establishes a causal connection to other decisions.
8. Do not reopen old decisions merely because they are old, untested, or improvable in theory.

## 2. Universal Law & Effect Principle

**ARGO Law — No Effect Without Governing Regularity:**

No observed effect is to be treated as lawless or causeless merely because ARGO does not yet know the governing law, mechanism, or relationship. The current unknown is ARGO's knowledge of the regularity, not permission to invent an explanation.

Required discipline:
1. separate observation from interpretation;
2. search for plausible mechanism/relationship/constraint;
3. compare patterns and causal candidates;
4. design the smallest discriminating experiment;
5. record supported law/pattern with scope/confidence;
6. otherwise retain `Mystery / Unresolved Effect`;
7. revisit only when new evidence/capability/hypothesis gives a reason;
8. do not loop without a new trigger.

### Mystery Record Minimum

Record Effect ID, context, date/checkpoint, known inputs/outputs, expected versus observed, tested/rejected explanations, current unknowns, cross-domain patterns, next reconsideration trigger and confidence/evidence level.

Unknown law means unknown to ARGO, not evidence-free permission to invent a law.

## 3. Solution State

Material solutions may be:
- `PRACTICAL-NOW`
- `IMPROVEMENT-CANDIDATE`
- `UNDER-REVIEW`
- `STABLE`
- `RETIRED/SUPERSEDED`

Never label a solution `OPTIMAL` merely because one test passed.

## 4. Improvement Ledger

Each meaningful improvement records solution ID, version/iteration, date, maturity/build checkpoint, problem, prior limitation, change, evidence/test, new effects, remaining limits, practical-fitness impact and decision context.

Version count is historical evidence, not a quality score.

## 5. Fitness Before Optimization

Before rejecting a practical solution for a theoretical improvement, determine whether it solves the present problem, is safe, sufficiently simple, has understood residual risk and whether further improvement is worth the cost now.

If yes, it may remain `PRACTICAL-NOW` with an improvement marker.

## 6. Anti-Loop Gate

Before another optimization cycle state the concrete weakness, evidence, expected improvement and stop condition. If no material weakness or measurable benefit exists, pause optimization.

## 7. Targeted Decision Reconsideration Gate

Prior decisions may be reconsidered only when materially relevant to the current problem: causal connection, relevant weak assumption, missing material test, new effect, or realistic ability to change the current decision.

Before reopening, record Decision ID/context, current relevance, prior evidence/assumptions, missing evidence, expected value, review cost/scope and stop condition.

The unit of review should normally be the assumption/model that produced the decision. Uncertainty does not propagate to neighboring decisions without causal evidence.

Outcomes:
- `CONFIRMED`
- `REFINED`
- `MODIFIED`
- `SUPERSEDED`
- `INCONCLUSIVE`

## 8. Simulation Requirement

For material architectural/build solutions, GOV-024 simulation/effect analysis should be used before implementation when feasible. Unexpected effects trigger model review rather than automatic rejection.

## 9. Evolution as Learning Evidence

Evolution records may reveal recurring reasoning errors, missed-effect classes, simplification patterns, prediction improvements and changes in engineering judgment. Version count alone is not intelligence/progress evidence.

## 10. Authority Boundary

This protocol does not authorize production mutation and does not override GOV-013, integrity holds, evidence authority, or existing governance contracts.

## 11. Required Closure

Every optimization, targeted reconsideration, or material unresolved-effect cycle must close with:

`Problem → Prior Learning → Relevance Gate → Baseline/Assumption → Candidate → Simulation/Test → Effects → Law/Pattern or Mystery Record → Decision → Implementation (if authorized) → Verification → Evolution/Reconsideration Record → Stop/Next Trigger`
