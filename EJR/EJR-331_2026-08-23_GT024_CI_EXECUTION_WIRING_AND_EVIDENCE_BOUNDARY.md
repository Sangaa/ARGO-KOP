## Knowledge Delta

**KD-037 — Execution evidence is commit-bound.**

A successful workflow run proves execution for its checked-out identity. It cannot be silently promoted as proof for later mutations.

**KD-038 — Workflow wiring is implementation evidence, not execution evidence.**

A committed workflow step proves intended CI invocation. Only a current run/job/step result proves actual execution.

**KD-039 — Historical runtime artifacts remain useful without becoming current proof.**

Old artifacts can establish that the evidence channel itself exists and can reveal prior behavior, but they remain historical evidence unless their identity and applicability match the current claim.
