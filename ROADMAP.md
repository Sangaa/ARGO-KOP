# ARGO KOP

Knowledge Operating Platform

---

## Purpose

This roadmap defines the strategic evolution of ARGO KOP.

It describes the planned development stages of the platform while preserving architectural consistency and long-term vision.

The roadmap represents direction, not fixed deadlines.

Every stage builds upon the previous one.
