# CORE-000A — Platform Glossary

---

Document ID: CORE-000A

Version: 1.0

Status: Official

Classification: Core Reference

Owner: ARGO KOP

---

# Purpose

This glossary defines the official terminology used throughout the ARGO KOP repository.

Only these definitions shall be considered canonical.

---

# ARGO

The engineering methodology governing reasoning, knowledge organization, and decision making.

ARGO is independent of any specific AI model.

---

# KOP

Knowledge Operating Platform.

The platform implementing the ARGO methodology.

---

# Repository

The structured knowledge base containing the official documentation, governance, architecture, decisions, and lessons learned.

The Repository is the Single Source of Truth.

---

# Governance

The collection of rules, principles, and constraints that regulate reasoning, documentation, reviews, and repository evolution.

---

# Architecture

The structural design describing how ARGO components interact and evolve.

---

# Core

The foundational identity and operating principles of the platform.

---

# Knowledge

Verified information preserved for future reuse.

Knowledge is version controlled and traceable.

---

# Decision

A documented engineering choice supported by evidence and reasoning.

---

# Fact

Information directly verified through reliable evidence.

---

# Assumption

A reasonable interpretation that still requires verification.

Assumptions shall never be presented as facts.

---

# Hypothesis

A proposed explanation requiring investigation before acceptance.

---

# Operational Reality

The actual observed behavior of users, processes, and workflows.

Operational reality has priority over theoretical assumptions.

---

# Verified Assessment

An assessment explicitly stating:

- Inspection Scope
- Evidence Level
- Confidence Level
- Repository Coverage
- Assessment Type

---

# Confidence Level

High

Complete verification.

Medium

Partial verification supported by evidence.

Low

Limited evidence or preliminary analysis.

---

# Canonical

The official version approved by repository governance.

---

# Archive

Historical material preserved for traceability.

Archived documents are not authoritative.

---

# Implementation

A specific AI environment operating under ARGO governance.

Examples include:

- ChatGPT

- Gemini

Future implementations may exist without changing ARGO itself.

---

# Engineering Review

A structured examination of repository content intended to improve consistency, quality, and governance.

---

# Repository Drift

Any inconsistency between the canonical repository definition and the actual repository contents.
