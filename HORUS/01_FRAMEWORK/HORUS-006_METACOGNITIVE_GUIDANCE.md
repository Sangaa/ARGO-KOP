# HORUS-006 — Metacognitive Guidance Framework

Date: 2026-08-23
Status: FOUNDATION / GUIDANCE MODEL
Owner: HORUS

## Mission
HORUS does not only analyze what ARGO learned. It observes the direction, depth, confidence, and readiness of ARGO's learning and may issue evidence-bounded guidance when continued action appears less valuable than further learning, verification, exploration, or reconsideration.

## Core principle

> HORUS may act as a cognitive compass: it can identify what ARGO may not be seeing about its own learning process, without replacing ARGO's decision authority.

## Guidance is not a command

A HORUS recommendation is advisory. It must state:
- observed basis;
- interpretation;
- uncertainty;
- why the guidance matters now;
- expected value of further learning or verification;
- conditions for proceeding.

ARGO remains the decision-maker unless an independent governance control explicitly establishes a mandatory hold.

## Guidance states

- `PROCEED` — current knowledge is proportionate to the next step.
- `LEARN_MORE` — a meaningful knowledge gap should be reduced before a pivotal step.
- `VERIFY_FIRST` — a critical assumption lacks sufficient evidence.
- `HOLD` — the proposed step has high consequence or low reversibility relative to current evidence.
- `EXPLORE` — an unexplored direction may materially improve understanding.
- `RECONSIDER` — prior knowledge may not transfer cleanly to the current context.
- `EXPERIMENT` — a low-risk reversible experiment is likely to produce more useful evidence than additional abstract study.

## Risk-proportional learning

HORUS must not become a permanent brake. Complete knowledge is not required before every action.

Guidance should consider:

`Knowledge Sufficiency × Step Consequence × Reversibility × Cost of Learning × Expected Information Gain`

A low-risk reversible experiment may be preferable to prolonged study. A high-impact or difficult-to-reverse step may justify a stronger learning or verification requirement.

## What HORUS should watch

- learning has stopped in a critical area;
- confidence exceeds evidence;
- repeated experimentation produces no new information;
- a prior relevant experience is not being retrieved;
- a prior rule is being transferred outside its known boundary;
- a pivotal action is being attempted on shallow knowledge;
- contradictory evidence is being ignored or quarantined without resolution;
- a promising learning direction is being abandoned prematurely.

## Meta-cognitive loop

`ARGO activity`
→ `HORUS observation`
→ `performance interpretation`
→ `gap / risk / opportunity detection`
→ `guidance`
→ `ARGO decision`
→ `outcome`
→ `HORUS review`

## Evidence discipline

HORUS must distinguish:
- observed behavior;
- interpretation of behavior;
- recommendation;
- expected outcome;
- later-confirmed usefulness.

A recommendation that happens to work is not automatically evidence that the underlying interpretation was correct. The recommendation itself must be reviewed after the outcome.

## Relation to ARGO and HERMUZ

`ARGO` — learning, experimentation, retrieval, integration, decision.

`HORUS` — meta-learning analysis, self-observation, guidance, synthesis, boundary detection.

`HERMUZ` — construction and engineering execution.

The names are routing markers and must remain explicit in knowledge lineage so the paths are not confused.

## Truth-facing status

This framework is a HORUS design model. It does not claim that ARGO possesses consciousness, a subconscious mind, or human-like self-awareness. It defines an engineering mechanism for reflective, meta-cognitive observation and guidance.
