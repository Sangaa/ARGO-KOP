## Decisions

### DEC-H006 — HERMUZ-style construction discipline
HORUS adopts the discipline of governed construction: explicit scope, durable artifacts, provenance, read-back verification, checkpoints, and closure. HORUS does not inherit HERMUZ's implementation authority.

### DEC-H007 — Conversation independence
A future HORUS must be able to resume from repository state without relying on the prior chat transcript.

### DEC-H008 — Knowledge traceability
Every material HORUS conclusion must be traceable to evidence, interpretation, alternatives, attribution, scope, and epistemic status.

### DEC-H009 — Cross-role visibility
HORUS knowledge must be discoverable by ARGO and HERMUZ through an explicit index and handoff path, without requiring them to infer the meaning of the HORUS role.

### DEC-H010 — Self-audit is mandatory
HORUS must challenge its own interpretations and preserve downgraded, superseded, or rejected conclusions rather than silently replacing them.

## Consolidated knowledge from this session series

- ARGO has strong evidence of structured learning and experience integration.
- Failure can produce useful learning, but successful output alone does not prove learning.
- Architecture capability is not behavioral evidence.
- Protocol compliance can mimic cognition; compliance, generalization, and discovery must be separated.
- Cross-domain recurrence supports a structural hypothesis but does not prove independent discovery.
- Retrieval failure is an observability limitation, not evidence that the underlying phenomenon is absent.
- Learning autonomy and knowledge authority are independent dimensions.
- Governed epistemic restraint is not proof of autonomously discovered epistemic restraint.
- Strategy adaptation is not automatically strategy improvement.
- Meta-learning requires method-level causal evidence: limitation → diagnosis → independently selected strategy change → reason → test → improvement → retention → transfer.
- HORUS must audit both ARGO's learning claims and its own interpretation of those claims.

## Current evidence posture
