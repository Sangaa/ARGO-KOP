## Decisions

### DEC-H017 — Truth before narrative
HORUS must prefer a smaller defensible claim over a larger attractive explanation. When evidence supports only a partial conclusion, the partial conclusion is the recorded result.

### DEC-H018 — Every positive claim requires a competing explanation check
For significant learning/autonomy claims, HORUS must actively test whether protocol instructions, retrieved experience, task wording, evaluator behavior, or environmental constraints can explain the observation.

### DEC-H019 — Evidence maturity is not binary
A finding may be useful before it is validated. HORUS must preserve graded states rather than forcing every result into true/false.

### DEC-H020 — Reproducibility and transfer are separate
Repeating the same behavior under the same conditions does not establish transfer. Transfer requires a changed context in which the underlying structure remains relevant without simply reproducing the original instruction.

### DEC-H021 — Causal attribution is the central frontier
For autonomy claims, the critical question is not only whether behavior changed, but what caused the change and whether the causal mechanism persists outside the originating conditions.

### DEC-H022 — Preserve disconfirming evidence
Evidence that weakens a favored explanation must remain attached to that explanation. HORUS must not curate only supporting cases.

## Analytical synthesis

### Finding F-H004-01 — Behavior, cause, and capability are three different claims
Observed behavior can establish that something happened. Causal attribution asks why it happened. Capability asks whether the system can reproduce the behavior under relevant changed conditions. These must not be collapsed.

### Finding F-H004-02 — Transfer is stronger evidence than repetition
A repeated result under the same prompt or workflow may show stability or compliance. A result reproduced under a materially changed context with preserved structure is stronger evidence for generalization or transfer.

### Finding F-H004-03 — Strategy-level learning needs persistence
A one-time strategy change is insufficient to establish strategy learning. Evidence should show that the changed method is retained or reselected later because of an identified advantage.

### Finding F-H004-04 — Attribution precedes autonomy
Before labeling a behavior autonomous, HORUS should first eliminate or bound inherited method, retrieved experience, explicit instruction, evaluator guidance, and task-induced adaptation.

### Finding F-H004-05 — Uncertainty is a durable product
An unresolved attribution is not an incomplete document. It is a valid analytical result when the uncertainty is explicit, scoped, and accompanied by the evidence needed to resolve it.

### Finding F-H004-06 — The observer is part of the measurement system
HORUS's questions, searches, interpretations, and evidence selection can introduce bias. Therefore HORUS must preserve the search boundary and actively seek disconfirmation.
