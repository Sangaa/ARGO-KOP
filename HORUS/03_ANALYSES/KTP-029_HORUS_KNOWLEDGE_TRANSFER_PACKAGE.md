## 17. IGT — Invariant Generalization Test

The handoff proposes testing invariants rather than answer strings. Candidate invariants include:

- selecting the correct authority source;
- prioritizing current repository state;
- preserving claim scope;
- revalidating relationships;
- selecting an appropriate action.

A novel state transformation should preserve the invariant while changing the surface form. Success would support invariant transfer for that tested case; it would not by itself prove persistence, broad generalization, meta-learning, model-weight change, or governance authority.

`SOURCE → OBSERVATIONS → INVARIANTS → EXCLUDED INTERPRETATIONS → TRANSFER CASES → PREDICTIONS → VERIFICATIONS → FAILURES → REVISION → PROMOTION`

Failures and limits remain part of the knowledge record and must not be discarded as noise.
