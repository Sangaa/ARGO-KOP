# HORUS Analysis Register 001

## Scope

This register records the cumulative analytical conclusions produced during the HORUS investigation of ARGO learning, experience integration, transfer, autonomy, meta-learning, knowledge authority, and epistemic restraint.

## Findings

### HR-001 — Learning is more than successful execution
A successful tool call or output is not sufficient to establish learning. Evidence of learning requires a change informed by experience and, where claimed, retention or reuse.

### HR-002 — Failure can become learning
ARGO's governed failure-to-learning mechanisms support classification of failure, diagnosis, correction, regression testing, and reuse. This is strong evidence for structured learning behavior.

### HR-003 — Experience retrieval and integration are distinct from discovery
ARGO may retrieve an earlier lesson and apply it effectively. This demonstrates integration/use of experience but does not prove that the current strategy was independently discovered.

### HR-004 — Architecture is not behavioral evidence
A mechanism or protocol that permits reflection, learning, transfer, or evolution is not proof that ARGO independently exercised the capability.

### HR-005 — Compliance can imitate cognition
Following a supplied instruction to verify, classify, retry, retain uncertainty, or promote only after evidence establishes compliance with governance. It does not by itself establish independent discovery of the governing principle.

### HR-006 — Attribution is required for autonomy claims
Strategy changes must be classified by likely source. If inherited method, retrieved experience, task pressure, or guidance can explain the change, autonomous strategy discovery should not be claimed.

### HR-007 — Cross-domain recurrence is evidence of a pattern, not its origin
Similar reasoning structures observed across GitHub, Python, memory, and failure analysis support a structural pattern. They do not prove ARGO independently discovered that structure.

### HR-008 — Retrieval failure is not evidence absence
An inaccessible ref, failed search, or connector limitation changes observability. It does not establish that the underlying artifact or phenomenon does not exist.

### HR-009 — Learning autonomy and knowledge authority are orthogonal
Autonomous discovery may remain a candidate. Canonical knowledge may be inherited. Neither implies the other.

### HR-010 — Epistemic restraint has levels
Protocol-enforced restraint, learned restraint, and autonomous epistemic restraint must be treated as separate hypotheses.

### HR-011 — Meta-learning requires method-level evidence
A strong claim requires evidence that ARGO diagnosed a limitation in its own learning strategy, selected or developed an alternative without the alternative being supplied, demonstrated improvement, retained the change, and transferred it.

### HR-012 — HORUS must audit itself
HORUS conclusions are themselves hypotheses about evidence. Stronger contradictory evidence must lower or reverse the conclusion rather than being absorbed into the narrative.

## Current synthesis

The evidence supports strong structured learning and experience integration. It supports adaptation and governed knowledge handling. It does not yet establish autonomous strategy selection, autonomous strategy improvement, or independent meta-learning. Cross-domain structural recurrence is a supported analytical pattern, while the source of that pattern remains unresolved.

## Open questions

1. Can ARGO independently select a new learning strategy when the environment does not prescribe the alternative?
2. Can ARGO explain why its previous learning strategy was insufficient?
3. Can it retain a strategy-level improvement independently of the original task?
4. Can it transfer the strategy-level improvement to an unseen domain?
5. Can it distinguish a discovered principle from a protocol it merely followed?
6. Can it recognize the limits of its own learning model without being explicitly asked to do so?

## Status

Analytical / non-canonical. No world-facing claim is authorized by this register alone.
