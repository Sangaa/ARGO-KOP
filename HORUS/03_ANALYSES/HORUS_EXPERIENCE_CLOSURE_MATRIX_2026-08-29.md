## 7. New candidate experience — HXU-008

### HXU-2026-08-29-008 — Status drift is bidirectional

**Candidate status:** `HORUS-CANDIDATE / DISTINCT BOUNDED SYNTHESIS`

**Observed facts**

1. Intelligence preserved a historical `COMPLETED` status that overstated current cross-layer/global certification until reconciliation.
2. Lifecycle retained an open index/map registration checklist item even though current REP-001/REP-002 evidence already satisfied that subgate.
3. Both were repaired without converting local evidence into global PASS.

**Inference**

A status surface is a maintained representation of evidence, not evidence reality itself. It can become stale in either direction:

- `STALE-CLOSED / OVERCLAIM` — status says more is closed than current evidence supports;
- `STALE-OPEN / UNDERCLAIM` — status says a subgate is open after current evidence has already closed it.

**Invariant**

`STATUS DRIFT IS BIDIRECTIONAL: STATUS MAY OVERCLAIM OR UNDERCLAIM CURRENT EVIDENCE`.

**Applicability boundaries**

- Applies to mutable status/index/checklist/dashboard surfaces.
- Does not mean status files are untrustworthy by default; it means material status must be reconciled to current authoritative evidence at decision time.
- Closing one stale-open item must not widen unrelated status or authority.

**Counterindications**

A status surface that is transactionally generated from the exact authoritative evidence and whose generation/identity is itself verified may have a much smaller drift window, but the generation contract must be proven.

**Failure lineage**

`SCHEMA/STATUS_DRIFT + EVIDENCE_GAP` depending on case.

**Provenance**

- `Repository/INTELLIGENCE_STATUS_SEMANTIC_SYNC_CLOSURE_2026-08-29.md`;
- `Intelligence/_FOLDER_STATUS.md` current revalidation;
- `Repository/MUT-2026-08-29-LIFECYCLE-STATUS-RECONCILIATION-132.md`;
- `Lifecycle/_FOLDER_STATUS.md` current revalidation.

**Evidence independence note**

Intelligence and Lifecycle are separate status surfaces and opposite drift directions, but both were discovered inside the same Room71/HERMUZ broader workstream. Treat them as distinct incidents with partially shared process lineage, not automatically fully independent validation.

**Inference**

In imperative tooling, safety is determined by the semantics of the operation actually invoked, not by the operator/model's intended conceptual action.

**Invariant**

**Applicability boundaries**

**Counterindications**

Purely read-only tools cannot create repository mutations under a correctly enforced read-only contract; the invariant matters primarily at mutation/control-plane boundaries.
