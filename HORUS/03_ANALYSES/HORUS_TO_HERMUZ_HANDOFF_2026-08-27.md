## 3. Constructs received
CEA — Causal Evidence Axis: separates outcome, novel transfer, baseline delta, attribution controls, and repeated independent improvement.

LAL — Learning Attribution Ledger: records what happened, why it is attributed, competing explanations, and remaining unknowns.

CLC — Counterfactual Learning Check: compares baseline/general-rule/learning-exposed conditions on a new case.

KUT — Knowledge Utilization Test: recall, application, transfer, improvement.

BLT — Boundary Learning Test: tests where a rule applies, stops applying, and whether the boundary is reused.

HST — Structural Transfer: separates surface match, case similarity, structural similarity, invariant transfer, strategy transfer, and strategy adaptation.

SAT / ALT — Strategy Adaptation Test / Adaptation Learning Test: test mismatch detection, adaptation, verification, and reuse of adaptation.

KTP — Knowledge Transfer Package: packages evidence, observations, inference, constructs, tests, limitations, confidence, provenance, target, verification requirements, and checkpoint.

KPT — Knowledge Portability Test: tests whether another executor can reconstruct known, inferred, unproven, boundary, test, and promotion constraints.

KRR — Knowledge Reconstruction & Recovery: reconstructs evidence → claim → invariant → boundary → strategy → uncertainty → required test, while tracking loss of evidence, context, boundary, uncertainty, provenance, and decision.
