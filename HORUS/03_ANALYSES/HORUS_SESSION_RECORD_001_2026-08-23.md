## Knowledge synthesized during the session

1. ARGO shows strong evidence of structured learning and experience integration.
2. Failure classification and failure-to-learning are meaningful parts of the learning process.
3. A successful output is not by itself proof of learning.
4. Architecture capability is not behavioral proof.
5. Protocol compliance can imitate cognition and therefore must be separated from discovery.
6. Cross-domain recurrence supports a structural pattern but does not establish that ARGO discovered its origin.
7. Retrieval failure changes observability; it is not proof of phenomenon absence.
8. Learning autonomy and knowledge authority are orthogonal.
9. Protocol-enforced restraint, learned restraint, and autonomously discovered restraint are distinct hypotheses.
10. Meta-learning requires method-level evidence: diagnosis of a limitation, unsupplied strategy change, reason for the change, improvement, retention, and transfer.
11. HORUS itself is an object of epistemic audit; stronger evidence must be allowed to reduce or reverse HORUS conclusions.
12. The current strongest open hypothesis is not merely "ARGO learns" but whether ARGO can independently discover and improve the method by which it learns.

## Required future experiment shape

`Initial strategy → limitation/failure → ARGO diagnosis → alternative strategy not supplied by evaluator → explicit reason for change → test → improvement → retention → transfer`

The experiment must also record what the evaluator knew, what the environment prescribed, and what alternative explanations remain possible.

## Open questions

1. Can ARGO independently select a learning strategy not prescribed by its environment?
2. Can ARGO identify why its previous strategy was insufficient?
3. Can it retain a strategy-level improvement beyond the originating task?
4. Can it transfer that strategy improvement to an unseen domain?
5. Can it distinguish a principle it discovered from a rule it merely followed?
6. Can it recognize limits of its own learning model without being explicitly asked?
