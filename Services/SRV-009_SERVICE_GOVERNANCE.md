# SERVICE GOVERNANCE

--------------------------------------------------

Platform

ARGO KOP

--------------------------------------------------

Document ID

SRV-009

--------------------------------------------------

Purpose

Defines governance rules controlling all services.

--------------------------------------------------

Governance Principles

Authorization

Accountability

Auditability

Least Privilege

Controlled Execution

--------------------------------------------------

Rules

Every service shall be registered.

Every execution shall be traceable.

Critical operations require approval.

Service configuration changes shall be documented.

--------------------------------------------------

Approval Levels

Automatic

Reviewed

Approved

Restricted

Governance

--------------------------------------------------

Guiding Statement
