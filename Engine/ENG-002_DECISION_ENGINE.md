# Purpose

The Decision Engine (`ENG-002`) transforms logical understanding generated by `ENG-001` into deterministic, risk-evaluated operational decisions. 

It evaluates decision candidates against platform policies, governance constraints, cost-benefit trade-offs, and risk thresholds to output canonical execution instructions.

+-----------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                    INPUT FROM REASONING ENGINE                        |

|  - Routes instructions to Execution Engine (ENG-006)                  |

---

# Core Operating Rules

1. **Compliance Superiority:** No decision shall be committed if it violates any governance policy in `Governance/` or quality gate in `Quality/`.
2. **Deterministic Selection:** Given identical inputs, constraints, and risk scores, the engine MUST output the exact same decision.
3. **Traceable Rationale:** Every decision must log its selection rationale and evaluated alternatives directly to `Logs/`.
