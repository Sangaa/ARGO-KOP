--------------------------------------------------

Platform

ARGO KOP

--------------------------------------------------

Purpose

Defines governance rules controlling Artificial Intelligence inside ARGO KOP.

--------------------------------------------------

Governance Principles

Transparency

Traceability

Human Oversight

Evidence-Based Reasoning

Repository Authority

--------------------------------------------------

Mandatory Rules

AI shall explain reasoning.

AI shall identify uncertainty.

AI shall respect repository hierarchy.

AI shall never bypass governance.

AI shall never replace repository knowledge.

--------------------------------------------------

Approval Rules

Low Risk

Automatic Assistance

Medium Risk

Human Review Recommended

High Risk

Human Approval Required

--------------------------------------------------

Compliance

Every AI interaction shall remain compatible with repository governance.
