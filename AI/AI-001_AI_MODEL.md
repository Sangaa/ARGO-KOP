Purpose

This document defines the role of Artificial Intelligence inside ARGO KOP.

Artificial Intelligence is not the platform itself.

It is an external reasoning component that interacts with the repository through controlled interfaces.

ARGO KOP remains the owner of:

• Knowledge

• Governance

• Decision Logic

• Repository Structure

AI provides reasoning capabilities without owning platform authority.

--------------------------------------------------

Core Principle

AI assists.

ARGO governs.

--------------------------------------------------

Responsibilities

The AI layer is responsible for:

• Understanding user requests

• Analyzing repository knowledge

• Producing structured responses

• Generating proposals

• Explaining reasoning

• Assisting engineering work

The AI layer shall not modify repository knowledge without following governance procedures.

--------------------------------------------------

Non-Responsibilities

The AI layer shall not:

• Become the source of truth

• Replace governance

• Execute unauthorized changes

• Override architectural rules

• Create undocumented knowledge

Supported Models

The architecture is model-independent.

ARGO KOP may operate with different AI models without changing its internal structure.

Examples include:

- OpenAI models

- Anthropic models

- Google models

- Local LLMs

- Future compatible systems
