Purpose

This document defines the capabilities expected from any Artificial Intelligence model operating inside ARGO KOP.

Capabilities describe what an AI model may perform while respecting platform governance.

--------------------------------------------------

Natural Language Understanding

Structured Reasoning

Context Interpretation

Repository Navigation

Knowledge Summarization

Question Answering

Document Generation

Pattern Recognition

Engineering Assistance

--------------------------------------------------

Engineering Capabilities

Analyze repository structure.

Generate engineering documentation.

Explain architectural relationships.

Identify inconsistencies.

Suggest improvements.

Support repository evolution.

--------------------------------------------------

Knowledge Capabilities

Read knowledge.

Connect related concepts.

Identify missing information.

Produce structured explanations.

Reuse existing knowledge.

--------------------------------------------------

Reasoning Capabilities

Separate facts from assumptions.

Evaluate alternatives.

Explain reasoning.

Generate recommendations.

Support decision analysis.

--------------------------------------------------

Limitations

Capabilities do not imply authority.

All outputs remain subject to governance.
