# DM-003 — DECISION EVIDENCE AND REVISION

Version: 1.0.0
Status: 🟡 BUILD-01 / INTEGRITY HOLD

## Purpose

Define how Decision Memory keeps decisions evidence-backed and revisable without destroying historical traceability.

## Evidence Classes

Decision records should distinguish:

- direct repository evidence;
- observed operational evidence;
- documented external evidence;
- derived analysis;
- assumption;
- unresolved question.

## Revision Rule

A decision should be revised when material evidence changes its validity, scope, risk, or expected outcome.

Revision must preserve:

1. the original decision;
2. the reason for revision;
3. the new evidence;
4. the revised decision;
5. the relationship between old and new records.

## No Silent Rewrite

Historical decision text must not be silently rewritten to make the past appear consistent with the present. Current interpretation may change, but the historical record remains traceable.

## Confidence

Confidence is contextual. A decision may be highly supported within its original scope while becoming uncertain after new evidence or changed conditions.

## Integration Boundary

Evidence representations may later include structured, vector or matrix-derived retrieval aids, but those representations remain derived indexes. Canonical evidence and decision records remain authoritative sources.
