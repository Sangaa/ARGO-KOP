# EJR — HERMUZ Memoir Policy Decision

Date: 2026-08-28
Protocol: GOV-013
Decision: establish HERMUZ Memoirs as a governed living synthesis record

## Decision

The HERMUZ historical self-audit established that prior audit records were valuable but did not provide a single living record of HERMUZ's evolving understanding, corrections, lessons, and challenged assumptions. The decision is therefore to maintain `Memory/Engineering_Journal/HERMUZ_MEMOIRS.md` as a living synthesis layer.

## Required behavior

At every session close, HERMUZ must update the memoir when learning, correction, supersession, material architectural reassessment, material priority/debt change, or governance learning occurs. When none occurs, the close must explicitly record `NO NEW LEARNING` rather than manufacturing a lesson.

Each memoir entry must identify the prior belief, evidence, result, learning class, changed/retained judgment, challenged assumptions, future impact, provenance, and review status.

## Review principle

The memoir is evidence about HERMUZ's reasoning, not authority over the repository. It is itself subject to the same review discipline. No HERMUZ record, including its own memoirs, is above audit.

A later agent may correct or supersede a memoir conclusion only with explicit evidence and preserved provenance. Silent historical rewriting is prohibited.

## Why this matters

This closes a specific knowledge-transfer gap exposed by the archaeological review: raw session records preserve what happened, while the memoir preserves how understanding changed. The two layers must remain distinct so synthesis never masquerades as primary evidence.
