Purpose

The Engineering Journal records the complete engineering history of ARGO KOP.

Every development session is documented to preserve:

- Architectural decisions
- Repository evolution
- Lessons learned
- Major discussions
- Build progress
- Future considerations

The journal provides a chronological engineering history of the platform.
