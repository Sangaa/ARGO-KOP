# HM-003 — HISTORICAL RETRIEVAL AND RELEVANCE

Version: 1.0.0
Status: 🟡 BUILD-01 / INTEGRITY HOLD

## Purpose

Define how historical material can be retrieved for context and learning without being mistaken for current truth.

## Retrieval Layers

Historical retrieval should expose, where applicable:

1. the original historical record;
2. its provenance;
3. its temporal scope;
4. later interpretations;
5. current relevance;
6. conflicting or superseding evidence.

## Relevance

Historical relevance is contextual. A record may be highly relevant to understanding why a decision exists while being irrelevant as evidence for current operational reality.

## Current-State Guard

Before historical evidence is used to justify a current action, the system should check whether newer evidence supersedes it.

## Future Retrieval Aids

Structured indexes, embeddings, vectors or matrices may improve historical retrieval later. Such derived retrieval aids must remain subordinate to the canonical historical record and provenance.

## Boundary

This artifact defines retrieval behavior, not current-state authority.
