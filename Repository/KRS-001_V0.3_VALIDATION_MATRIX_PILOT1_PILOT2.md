## Findings
1. v0.3 resolves serialization drift by defining one canonical envelope.
2. v0.3 correctly exposes the strongest unresolved weakness: Pilot 2 cannot claim immutable source provenance from `main` alone.
3. v0.3 must not silently backfill a blob SHA from an assumed ref; it must be retrieved and recorded from the exact source ref.
4. v0.3 is capable of representing both pilots, but Pilot 1 and Pilot 2 must be normalized before they can be considered conformant objects.
5. This validation does NOT authorize source replacement or bulk migration.

## Decision
