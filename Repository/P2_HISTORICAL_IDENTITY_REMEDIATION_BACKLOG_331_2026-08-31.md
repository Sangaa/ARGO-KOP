`RECONSTRUCT FIRST VALID ALLOCATION → CHECK STRONGER CONTRARY EVIDENCE → PROVE REPLACEMENT VACANCY OVER COMPLETE REACHABLE HISTORY → ENUMERATE EXACT-PATH + SEMANTIC CONSUMERS → PREWRITE MATRIX → ONE MATERIAL IDENTITY REPAIR → READ-BACK → INTERNAL-ID + FULL-STACK → CLOSE`.

The first-valid-allocation rule established by Plan 204 is not automatically generalized where stronger group-specific provenance contradicts or fails to establish the allocation history. Reverse-direction, compound and multi-transition groups keep their special evidence boundaries.
