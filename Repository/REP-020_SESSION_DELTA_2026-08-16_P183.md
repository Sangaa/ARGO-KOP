# REP-020 — Session Delta P183

## Purpose
Record the next growth target for ARGO KOP after the 2026-08-16 market/value assessment and establish the practical learning-to-product objective.

## Baseline
The prior benchmark recorded ARGO as:
- Concept: 9/10
- Architecture: 9/10
- Engineering maturity: 8.5/10
- Build methodology: 9.5/10
- Testing/evidence: 8.5/10
- Product readiness: 4.5/10
- Market opportunity: 9/10
- Potential differentiation: 9/10

These scores are internal maturity indicators, not audited financial valuations.

## New Strategic Objective
After completion of the core build, ARGO will be taught Kotlin, Android Studio, and Android application development through a real product build.

The first Android product is intended to serve three purposes simultaneously:
1. prove ARGO can learn and apply a new technical domain;
2. provide a practical end-to-end demonstration of ARGO's reasoning, governance, evidence, and engineering discipline;
3. become a potential commercial/funding asset for ARGO if the resulting product demonstrates real user value.

## Governance Rule
The Android learning/build phase must NOT begin before the core ARGO build is declared complete under the governing build protocol. Learning must be evidence-driven and recorded as a progression of knowledge, tests, mistakes, corrections, and verified capabilities.

## Product Proof Target
Target progression:

Engineering Proof
→ Learning Proof
→ Product Proof
→ User Proof
→ Revenue/Funding Proof

The Android application is therefore treated as a future Product Proof vehicle, not as a substitute for completing the current architecture.

## Current Objectives
### O1 — Complete Core Spine
Close remaining canonical seams without weakening evidence, authorization, or integrity boundaries.

### O2 — Reach Product Readiness
Raise the current product-readiness benchmark from 4.5/10 toward 7/10+ by demonstrating a coherent usable product boundary, not by adding documentation volume.

### O3 — Learning Capability
After core completion, establish a governed Kotlin/Android learning track with measurable milestones and executable examples.

### O4 — First Android Product
Build one complete Android application from requirements through architecture, Kotlin implementation, testing, packaging, and documented release evidence.

### O5 — Commercial Proof
Use the application to test user demand, measurable value, willingness to pay, and/or credible funding interest. No funding or valuation claim is considered proven until external evidence exists.

## Anti-Overbuild Rule
The future Android project must reuse ARGO capabilities wherever appropriate and must not create a parallel cognitive architecture merely for demonstration.

## Success Criteria
A future learning/product milestone is considered successful only when:
- the capability is implemented;
- it is tested;
- the result is reproducible;
- the learning is recorded;
- the limitation is known;
- the resulting product provides measurable user value.

## Current State
Core build remains the priority. Android/Kotlin is a post-core target. Integrity remains HOLD until the canonical completion criteria are satisfied.

## P183 Decision
The future Android application is now an explicit strategic target for ARGO's transition from engineering proof to product proof and eventual revenue/funding proof, while preserving the existing build protocol and evidence boundaries.

End of P183.
