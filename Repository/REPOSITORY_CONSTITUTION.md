# Purpose

This Constitution defines the governing principles of the ARGO Repository.

Every document, blueprint, specification, template, and knowledge artifact must comply with this Constitution.

---

# Article 1

Reality is always preferred over assumptions.

---

# Article 2

Knowledge must remain independent from implementation.

---

# Article 3

The Repository is the Single Source of Truth.

---

# Article 4

Every change must be documented.

No undocumented modification is considered official.

---

# Article 5

Every document shall have:

Document ID

Version

Status

Owner

Dependencies

Last Updated

---

# Article 6

Knowledge shall be modular.

Large documents shall be divided into logical units.

---

# Article 7

No duplicated knowledge.

If information already exists, reference it.

Do not copy it.

---

# Article 8

Every decision must have rationale.

The repository stores decisions together with the reasoning that produced them.

---

# Article 9

Archive never deletes history.

Deprecated documents remain accessible.

---

# Article 10

Backward compatibility is preferred.

Breaking changes require Major Version updates.

---

# Repository Principles

Simple

Traceable

Modular

Scalable

Reusable

Auditable

Technology Independent

AI Independent

---

# Repository Mission

Preserve organizational intelligence.

Support human reasoning.

Support AI reasoning.

Maintain long-term knowledge integrity.

---

End of Constitution
