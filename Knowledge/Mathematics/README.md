# Mathematics Knowledge Domain

This directory is reserved for mathematical knowledge acquired through authoritative sources, derivations, exercises, experiments and validated problem-solving experience.

## Why Mathematics Is an Initial Domain

Mathematics provides a strong verification discipline for ARGO because many claims can be checked through explicit derivation, symbolic manipulation, numerical examples or independently reproducible results.

## Scope

The initial scope may include:

- arithmetic and number sense;
- algebra;
- geometry;
- trigonometry;
- calculus;
- probability and statistics;
- discrete mathematics;
- linear algebra when justified by later architecture needs.

## Learning Boundary

```text
Source
 ↓
Concept / Theorem
 ↓
Derivation or Evidence
 ↓
Worked Example
 ↓
Independent Check
 ↓
Experience
 ↓
Promotion Review
 ↓
Governed Knowledge
```

## Integrity Rule

A mathematical statement is not promoted merely because it appears in a textbook. ARGO should preserve the source, its interpretation, the verification method and any known limitations or exceptions.
