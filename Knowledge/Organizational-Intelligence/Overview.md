## Domain Purpose

This domain consolidates knowledge about building and maintaining organizational intelligence - the collective capability of an organization to understand itself, learn, and make better decisions.

## Domain Scope

### Included Topics:
- Organizational learning
- Knowledge sharing practices
- Culture and structure effects
- Performance measurement
- Continuous improvement
- Organizational memory
- Change management

## Key Concepts

### 1. Organizational Learning
- Single-loop: Improve within existing framework
- Double-loop: Question and change framework
- Triple-loop: Examine learning process itself

### 2. Knowledge Sharing Effectiveness
- Depends on psychological safety
- Requires clear channels
- Needs incentive alignment

### 3. Organizational Memory
- Capturing institutional knowledge
- Preventing loss when people leave
- Enabling faster decision-making
