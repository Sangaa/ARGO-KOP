## Domain Purpose

## Key Frameworks

### 1. First Principles Thinking
- Break complex problems into fundamental elements
- Rebuild solutions from basics
- Avoids inherited assumptions

### 2. Systems Thinking
- Understand interconnections and relationships
- Recognize feedback loops
- Predict emergent properties

### 3. Analogical Reasoning
- Transfer knowledge from known to unknown
- Identify structural similarities
- Enable creative problem-solving

### 4. Root Cause Analysis
- Dig beneath surface symptoms
- Identify true sources of problems
- Enable durable solutions
