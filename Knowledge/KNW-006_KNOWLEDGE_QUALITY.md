# Purpose

This document defines the quality model used to evaluate every knowledge asset stored within ARGO KOP.

Knowledge Quality ensures that repository knowledge remains reliable, reusable and suitable for long-term organizational intelligence.

---

# Objectives

Knowledge Quality shall:

- Improve knowledge reliability.
- Maintain repository trust.
- Increase reasoning quality.
- Support governance.
- Preserve long-term maintainability.

---

# Knowledge Quality Philosophy

Knowledge quality is measured.

It is never assumed.

Every knowledge object shall satisfy measurable quality criteria before becoming authoritative.

---

# Quality Dimensions

Knowledge shall be evaluated using the following dimensions:

Accuracy

Completeness

Consistency

Clarity

Relevance

Traceability

Maintainability

Reusability

Authority

Timeliness

---

# Accuracy

Knowledge shall represent verified reality.

Unsupported assumptions shall never be classified as authoritative knowledge.

---

# Completeness

Knowledge shall contain sufficient information for its intended purpose.

Incomplete knowledge shall be clearly identified.

---

# Consistency

Knowledge shall not contradict:

Core

Governance

Architecture

Repository

Approved Decisions

---

# Clarity

Knowledge shall be:

Understandable

Unambiguous

Well Structured

Readable

---

# Relevance

Knowledge shall remain aligned with platform objectives.

Obsolete knowledge shall be reviewed.

---

# Traceability

Every knowledge object shall preserve:

Source

Owner

Version

Relationships

Approval History

---

# Maintainability

Knowledge shall be easy to:

Review

Update

Extend

Archive

---

# Reusability

Knowledge shall be reusable across:

Projects

Runtime

Reasoning

Documentation

Engineering Activities

---

# Authority

Repository-approved knowledge is authoritative.

Conversation context is temporary.

Repository always prevails.

---

# Timeliness

Knowledge shall be periodically reviewed.

Outdated knowledge shall be revised or archived.

---

# Quality Review

Knowledge Quality Reviews shall verify:

Accuracy

Completeness

Consistency

Relationships

Traceability

Repository Alignment

Architecture Alignment

Governance Compliance

---

# Repository Integrity

Knowledge quality protects:

Architecture

Repository

Governance

Reasoning

Decision Making

Future Evolution
