**Statement:** ARGO is a structured cognitive framework for knowledge preservation and reasoning, fundamentally different from AI systems.

**Distinctions:**
- **vs. AI:** ARGO provides frameworks; AI provides inferences
- **vs. Chatbot:** ARGO is knowledge platform; chatbots are conversational
- **vs. Search:** ARGO organizes meaning; search finds keywords
- **Key Difference:** ARGO output is deterministic based on structure; AI output is probabilistic

**Application:** ARGO complements AI systems, doesn't replace them
