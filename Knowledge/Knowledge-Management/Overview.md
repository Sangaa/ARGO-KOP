## Domain Purpose

This domain consolidates best practices and verified facts about managing knowledge systems effectively, including organization, validation, maintenance, and evolution.

- Classification and taxonomy

- Knowledge lifecycle management

- Repository management

## Key Concepts

### 1. Knowledge Lifecycle
- **Creation** - Knowledge generated
- **Validation** - Facts verified
- **Organization** - Structured and catalogued
- **Access** - Retrieved and used
- **Maintenance** - Updated and refreshed
- **Evolution** - Refined over time
- **Archival** - Superseded content preserved

### 2. Quality Tiers
- **Verified** - Multiple sources confirmed
- **Provisional** - Single source, awaiting confirmation
- **Exploratory** - Early stage, under investigation
- **Archived** - Superseded, kept for reference

### 3. Organization Levels
- **Domain** - Major knowledge area
- **Subdomain** - Specialized area
- **Topic** - Specific subject
- **Concept** - Detailed element
