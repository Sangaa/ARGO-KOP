## Key Research Sources

### Decision Science
1. **"Thinking, Fast and Slow" by Daniel Kahneman**
   - Covers cognitive biases in decision-making
   - Evidence for System 1 vs System 2 thinking
   - Extensively cited in behavioral decision science

2. **"Decisive" by Chip & Dan Heath**
   - WRAP framework for decision-making
   - Research on decision process improvement
   - Practical application frameworks

3. **"Good Strategy, Bad Strategy" by Richard Rumelt**
   - Strategic decision frameworks
   - Gap identification and leverage
   - Implementation challenges

### Organizational Behavior
4. **"Wisdom of Crowds" by James Surowiecki**
   - Collective intelligence principles
   - Conditions for effective group decisions
   - Diversity and independence factors

5. **Harvard Business Review**
   - Regular decision-making research
   - Organizational studies
   - Multi-stakeholder decision dynamics

### Project Management
6. **PMBOK Guide (Project Management Institute)**
   - Decision documentation standards
   - Implementation risk assessment
   - Resource allocation frameworks
