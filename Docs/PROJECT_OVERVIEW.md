--------------------------------------------------

# What is ARGO KOP?

ARGO KOP is a Knowledge Operating Platform.

It is designed to organize knowledge, preserve architectural consistency, support structured decision-making, and provide a stable foundation for future intelligent systems.

ARGO KOP is not a chatbot.

ARGO KOP is not an AI model.

ARGO KOP is the platform that governs how knowledge is organized and managed.

--------------------------------------------------

# Project Goals

Create a reusable knowledge platform.

Provide a structured repository.

Separate knowledge from implementation.

Support long-term evolution.

Maintain architectural integrity.
