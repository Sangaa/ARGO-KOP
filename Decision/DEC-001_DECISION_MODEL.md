Purpose

This document defines the official decision model used throughout ARGO KOP.

The model establishes how decisions are created, evaluated, approved, documented, and reviewed.

The objective is to ensure that every decision is logical, traceable, evidence-based, and aligned with the platform constitution.

--------------------------------------------------

Decision Philosophy

ARGO KOP does not make decisions based on authority, popularity, or assumptions.

Every decision must be supported by:

• Facts

• Evidence

• Analysis

• Repository Knowledge

• Governance Rules

--------------------------------------------------

Decision Principles

Reality Before Theory

Evidence Before Opinion

Traceability Before Complexity

Governance Before Execution

Human Approval for High-Impact Decisions
