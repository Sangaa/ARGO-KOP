Purpose

Ensures that every decision can be traced back to its origin.

Objectives

Support auditing.

Enable future reviews.

Preserve engineering context.

Improve accountability.

--------------------------------------------------

Rules

Every decision shall have a unique identifier.

Every revision shall be documented.

No anonymous decisions.

Guiding Statement

If a decision cannot be traced, it cannot be trusted.
