Purpose

This document defines the official lifecycle of every decision inside ARGO KOP.

Every decision shall follow the same controlled process to ensure consistency, traceability, and governance compliance.

--------------------------------------------------

Lifecycle Stages

Stage 1

Problem Identification

Define the actual problem.

Separate facts from assumptions.

Avoid proposing solutions before understanding the problem.

--------------------------------------------------

Stage 2

Context Collection

Collect all available context from:

Repository

Memory

Knowledge

Projects

Runtime

--------------------------------------------------

Stage 3

Evidence Collection

Gather supporting evidence.

Evidence must be verifiable whenever possible.

--------------------------------------------------

Stage 4

Knowledge Analysis

Compare current knowledge.

Identify previous similar decisions.

Detect dependencies.

--------------------------------------------------

Stage 5

Alternative Generation

Produce multiple solutions.

Avoid single-solution thinking.

--------------------------------------------------

Stage 6

Risk Assessment

Evaluate:

Technical Risk

Architectural Risk

Repository Impact

Governance Impact

Long-Term Impact

--------------------------------------------------

Stage 7

Decision Proposal

Prepare a structured recommendation including:

Reasoning

Benefits

Risks

Alternatives

Evidence

--------------------------------------------------

Stage 8

Approval

Low-impact decisions may be approved automatically according to governance.

High-impact decisions require human approval.

--------------------------------------------------

Stage 9

Execution

Execute only the approved decision.

--------------------------------------------------

Stage 10

Validation

Verify that execution matches the approved decision.

--------------------------------------------------

Stage 11

Documentation

Record:

Decision

Reasoning

Evidence

Outcome

--------------------------------------------------

Stage 12

Knowledge Update

Store lessons learned.

Update repository knowledge when necessary.

--------------------------------------------------

Decision Rules

No execution before approval.

No approval without evidence.

No undocumented decisions.

No hidden modifications.
