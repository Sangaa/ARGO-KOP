Purpose

Defines the official evaluation criteria used by ARGO KOP when comparing decision alternatives.

--------------------------------------------------

Evaluation Criteria

• Evidence Strength

• Architectural Consistency

• Governance Compliance

• Repository Impact

• Knowledge Reusability

• Technical Feasibility

• Operational Simplicity

• Long-Term Sustainability

• Risk Level

• Human Value

--------------------------------------------------

Scoring Principle

Each criterion should be evaluated objectively.

Higher evidence and lower complexity are preferred.

--------------------------------------------------

Decision Rule

A decision shall never be selected based solely on convenience.
